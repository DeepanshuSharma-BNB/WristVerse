Welcome to WristVerse

Explore the solar system through perspective, time and motion. Everything happens directly in your browser.

Rotate your wrist

Tap WRIST, allow sensor access, then twist or tilt your arm to orbit the scene.

Swipe left or right

Move between planets. On desktop, use ← and →.

Pinch / Enter

Inspect the selected planet. Pinch again to return.

Recenter

Hold your wrist in a comfortable position, then select RECENTER.

If wrist tracking is unavailable, drag the scene with a finger or mouse. This app never simulates live sensor readings.
